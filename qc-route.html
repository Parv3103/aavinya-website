01 / IDENTITY

Who We Are

Established under the Department of Artificial Intelligence at J D College of Engineering & Management, Nagpur, AAVINYA brings together students across all years into one technical community. The forum is run by an elected Admin Body and twelve specialised committees that organise research tracks, build labs, competitions, workshops and expert talks through the academic session.

  • Student-led, faculty-guided AI forum
  • 12 committees with a clear working hierarchy
  • Research, projects and industry exposure
  • Responsible and ethical use of AI
